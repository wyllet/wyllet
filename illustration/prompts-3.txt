WYLLET BLOG COVERS (4 images)
Reference image: attach logo.png for the character. These are editorial covers, not ads.

GLOBAL STYLE (apply to all 4)
Wide editorial cover, 2400 x 1260 (about 1.9:1), for a developer blog. Calm, premium, minimal, lots of negative space on the left half for a title that will be added later in code (keep the left 45% empty and evenly lit). Background: deep ink green #06110F with a very soft radial mint glow (#48ECD2 at 15% opacity) behind the subject. The Wyllet mascot (identical to the reference: matte mint vinyl wallet-box with a hinged lid and two black pill eyes, no mouth) sits in the right half. Physical, tactile objects with a soft matte finish, studio lighting from the upper left, soft shadows, shallow depth of field. Colour palette limited to ink #06110F, mint #48ECD2, lime #C6F432 as a tiny accent, off-white #F1F8F6.
Do not add: any text or numbers, real brand logos, coins, bitcoin or ethereum symbols, blockchain cubes, circuit boards, holograms, neon grids, chrome, lens flares, purple-blue gradients, busy backgrounds, people.
