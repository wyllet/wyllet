# Brand asset sources

Official artwork bundled with Wyllet, embedded as data URIs in
`src/wallets/brandIcons.ts`, `src/chains/brandIcons.ts` and `src/tokens/brandIcons.ts`.
The files in this folder are the unmodified originals, kept for provenance; they are not published to npm.
Marks are unaltered. Transparent marks are centered on a plain white tile so they stay visible on dark themes.
All names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify supported wallets, networks and tokens.
